Media & Broadcasting

Alongside his academic work, Dr. Kapchits has spent much of his professional life in broadcasting, and part of what he has written about Somalia was addressed not to scholars but to a radio audience. This page gathers what exists in that form.

Please note: there is at present no recorded series of video lectures. Rather than promise material that does not exist, this section documents the media work that does.

Radio

From 1967 to 1994 G. L. Kapchits worked in the Somali service of Radio Moscow, broadcasting in Somali to listeners in the Horn of Africa. From 1994 onwards he has worked as a commentator for the Voice of Russia.

Nearly three decades of daily broadcasting in Somali form the background to the linguistic work presented elsewhere on this site. The command of the spoken language on which the paremiological experiment and the grammatical studies rest was acquired and exercised in the studio as much as in the field.

The 2009 Journey to Somalia

In May 2009 Dr. Kapchits travelled to Somalia as interpreter and consultant to a film crew headed by Vladimir Sinelnikov, with Alexander Kublitsky as cameraman.

Out of that journey came a cycle of four travel essays, published by the Voice of Russia. They belong to the same genre as his other reportage — first-hand observation of the country and its people, written by someone who could speak to those he met in their own language.
