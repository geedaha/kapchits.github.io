About the author

Georgi Leonidovich Kapchits, born 2 October 1939 — linguist and folklorist working on the Somali language and its oral tradition. Candidate of Philological Sciences.

  • 1967 — graduated from the Institute of Oriental Languages, Moscow State University; qualified in Amharic and Somali.
  • 1967–1994 — announcer and translator in the Somali service of Radio Moscow’s World Service; from 1994 — commentator at the Voice of Russia.
  • 2000 — Candidate of Philological Sciences: a dissertation on the syntax of Somali (Institute of Asian and African Studies, Moscow State University; supervisor V. Ya. Porkhomovsky), published as a book in Aachen in 2005.
  • Taught Somali at Moscow State University, the Humboldt University and the Free University of Berlin.
  • 1989 Mogadishu, Congress of Somali Studies · 1999–2001 the paremiological experiment in Berlin and Aarhus (Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft; 45 informants, 656 proverbs) · 2001 Hargeisa, 8th International Congress of Somali Studies · 2003 Djibouti · 2009 Somalia, Somaliland and Puntland · 2012 Hargeisa International Book Fair.
  • Twelve books between 1983 and 2022 — proverbs, folktales and a grammar of the sentence particles — and the editing of G. L. Permyakov’s works (1988, 2001). See Books.
  • Languages: Russian, Somali, English; Amharic at university.
