Curriculum Vitae

Every entry below rests on a stated source: the author’s own curriculum vitae as published on kapchits.narod.ru, the imprints of his books, and the papers and reports held in this archive. Where a fact could not be established, the line is absent rather than guessed at.

Personal

Born 2 October 1939.

Languages. Russian (native); Somali; English. Qualified at university in Amharic alongside Somali.

Married, two daughters.

Education

1967 — graduated from the Institute of Oriental Languages, Moscow State University. Qualified as a linguist in the field of Amharic and Somali studies.

2000 — Candidate of Philological Sciences. Dissertation on the syntax of Somali, defended at the Institute of Asian and African Studies, Moscow State University, under the supervision of Prof. V. Ya. Porkhomovsky. Published as a book by Shaker Verlag, Aachen, in 2005.

Professional posts

1967–1994 — announcer and translator, Somali service of Radio Moscow’s World Service. The section was closed in 1994.

From 1994 — commentator, Information Department of the State Radio Company “Voice of Russia”.

Teaching

Lectured on the Somali language at the universities of Moscow and Berlin.

Travel and field work

1989 — Mogadishu, at the invitation of the Somali Academy of Sciences, for the Congress of Somali Studies.

1999–2001 — the paremiological experiment, conducted in Berlin (Humboldt University, 1999; Free University, 2001) and in Aarhus (2001), with the support of the Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft. Forty-five informants; 656 truncated proverbs.

2001 — Hargeisa, for the 8th International Congress of Somali Studies (3–13 July), with journeys to Borama, Berbera, Gabiley and Sheikh.

2003 — Djibouti.

2009 — Somalia, Somaliland and Puntland, in May, as interpreter and consultant to a Russian television crew.

2012 — Hargeisa, for the 5th International Book Fair.

Books

Nine books, 1983–2012, and a trilingual collection of folktales in 2016. Full imprints, with publishers, ISBNs and page counts, are given under Books.

Editorial work

G. L. Permyakov, Fundamentals of Structural Paremiology — compilation and introductory article, 1988.

Proverbs and Sayings of the Peoples of the East — editor, 2001.

Papers at congresses and conferences

The documented record, with the paper given at each, is set out under Conferences & Congresses. In outline:

  • 4th Congress of Somali Studies, Mogadishu, 1989
  • 8th International Congress of Somali Studies, Hargeisa, 2001
  • 4th International Conference of Cushitic and Omotic Languages, Leiden, 2003
  • 9th Congress of the Somali Studies International Association, Aalborg, 2004
  • 7th International Semito-Hamitic Congress, Berlin, 2004
  • Somali Studies International Association conference, Ohio, 2007
  • Buranbur conference, San Diego, 2007 — two papers
  • “5000 Years of Semito-Hamitic Languages in Asia and Africa”, Free University of Berlin, 2010
  • Somali Week Festival, London, 2011
  • 5th Hargeisa International Book Fair, 2012

Articles

Some twenty articles and papers, and reviews of the books. The bibliography, and the full texts of those that survive in the archive, are under Articles & Papers.

Research

The long-running lines of work — the paremiological experiment, the dictionary of proverbs, kinship terminology with V. Ya. Porkhomovsky, and the dialects of southern Somalia — are described under Research Projects.
