Publications

Dr. Kapchits has authored an extensive body of work spanning over four decades of research in Somalistics. His publications include groundbreaking monographs, peer-reviewed articles, and comprehensive folklore collections.

Categories

Major scholarly works including books on Somali proverbs, folktales, and linguistic analysis.

Proverbs

Comprehensive studies of Somali paremiology and proverbial wisdom.
