Shirarka iyo Shirweynayaasha

Afartan sano oo Cilmi-Soomaali ah oo inta badan laga sameeyay Moscow waxay ka dhigan tahay in cilmiga laftiisa lagu kulmi jiray shirweynayaasha — Muqdisho, Hargeysa, Leiden, Berlin, San Diego. Waxa soo socda waa diiwaanka la dukumentiyay ee ka-qaybgalladaas, iyo waraaqihii lagu akhriyay mid kasta.

Shirweynayaasha Daraasaadka Soomaaliyeed

Shirweynihii 4aad ee Daraasaadka Soomaaliyeed — Muqdisho, 1989. Booqashadii ugu horreysay ee Soomaaliya, iyadoo lagu casuumay Akadeemiyada Cilmiga ee Soomaaliyeed. Labaatan sano kadib, isla magaaladaas ayaa dib loo booqday, waxaana laga qoray taxane qoraallo safar ah oo loogu talo galay Codka Ruushka.

Shirweynihii 8aad ee Caalamiga ah ee Daraasaadka Soomaaliyeed — Hargeysa, 3–13 Luulyo 2001. Waraaq: Towards the paremiological minimum of the Somali language, oo soo bandhigaysa natiijooyinka tijaabinta goobta ee dhalisay liis ka kooban 226 maahmaah oo si firfircoon ay u yaqaaniin badankii wariyayaasha.

Shirweynaha laftiisa — abwaannadiisa, boqolkii waraaqood ee ku saabsanaa nabadda, maamulka iyo dib-u-dhiska, iyo dowladda dhallinta ah ee aan weli la aqoonsanayn ee martida u ahayd — waxaa si dheer looga hadlay qormada Aqoonyahanno Dalka Abwaannada, oo markii ugu horreysay lagu daabacay Horn of Africa, Vol. IXX, 2001.

Shirweynihii 9aad ee Ururka Caalamiga ah ee Daraasaadka Soomaaliyeed — 2004. Waraaq: Variants and Variations of Somali Proverbs (Some results of the paremiological experiment in Berlin and Aarhus), oo kala saaraysa variation — oo macnaha guud sidiisii uga tagta — iyo variant — oo macnaha beddela oo laga yaabo inuu maahmaah gooni ah noqdo.

Shirka Ururka Caalamiga ah ee Daraasaadka Soomaaliyeed — Ohio, 2007.

Luuqad-yaqaannimada Kushiitiga iyo Semito-Xaamitiga

Shirkii 4aad ee Caalamiga ah ee Luuqadaha Kushiitiga iyo Omotic — Leiden, 10–12 Abriil 2003. Waraaq: Some results of the Somali paremiological experiment in Berlin and Arhus — sharraxaadda ugu buuxda ee habka: sida ururinta tijaabinta looga soo ururiyay qaamuus ka kooban in ka badan 6,000 maahmaah, loona yareeyay 656 maahmaah oo la gooyay, kadibna loo dhiibay 45 wariye; iyo sida, 29,520 dhammaystirid oo suurtagal ah, 12,342 (41.8%) si sax ah loo buuxiyay.

Shirweynihii 7aad ee Caalamiga ah ee Semito-Xaamitiga — Berlin, 2004. Waraaq: Paremias without sentence particles, oo ku saabsan todobada boqolkiiba ee ururinta ee ay ka maqan yihiin xubnaha diiradda ee waajibka ah — iyo sababta ay tahay in qayb ka mid ah kooxdaas aan xubin gabi ahaanba lagu soo celin karin, taasoo maahmaahyadaas ka dhigaysa lakabkii ugu duugga weynaa ee kaydka maahmaahyada.

“5000 Sano oo Luuqado Semito-Xaamiti ah oo ku hadlay Aasiya iyo Afrika” — Jaamacadda Xorta ah ee Berlin, 22–24 Luulyo 2010. Waraaq ku saabsan kala qaybsanaanta lahjadaha Soomaaliga iyo xaaladda bulsho-luuqadeed ee gobolka koonfureed ee wax yar la baaray. Shirka waxaa lagu sharraxay xusuus-qorka A. G. Belova ee Vostok (Oriens), 2011, No. 1, bogagga 137–142.

Xafladaha iyo Munaasabadaha Dadweynaha

Shirka Buranbur — San Diego, 3 Maarso 2007. Laba waraaqood: On some peculiarities of the Somali language, proverbs and folktales, oo hordhac u ahaa luuqadda iyo hiddaha afkeeda oo loogu talo galay dhagaystayaal kala duwan; iyo Men and women in the Somali narrative folklore, oo isugu keenta maahmaahyada ku saabsan haweenka si markii hore u eg liis cabashooyin ah, kadibna dhinaca kale u rogta — Naagla’aani waa nafla’aan — kuna tijaabisa laba sheeko-dadweyne.

Xafladda Todobaadka Soomaalida — Oxford House, Bethnal Green, London, 22–30 Oktoobar 2011. Fiid ku saabsan sawir-gacmeedka iyo tilmaanta maahmaahyada, maahlaanka iyo sheekooyinka. Mawduuca xafladda sannadkaas wuxuu ahaa turjumaadda suugaanta. Munaasabadda waxaa akhristayaasha Soomaalida u soo tebiyay wariye Maxamed Xasan “Alto”.

Bandhiggii 5aad ee Caalamiga ah ee Buugaagta Hargeysa — Luulyo 2012. Soo bandhigidda buugga Soomaalidu Been ma Maahmaahdo 14 Luulyo 2012. Bandhigga iyo soo bandhigiddan waxaa daboolay The Guardian iyo wargeyska Horn — eeg Wararka.

Maahmaah-cilmi

Interdisciplinary Colloquium on Proverbs — Tavira, Portugal, 2018. Qoraal ku saabsan maahmaahyada tirooyinka ku dhisan. Waxaa xusay Peter Unseth faalladiisa qaamuuska 2020 — Proverbium, Mug 39, 2022. Kanu waa munaasabadda ugu dambeysa oo il loo helay; liiskii kor ku xusan, oo laga soo qaatay buug-tixraaca qoraaga, wuxuu ku dhammaaday 2012.
